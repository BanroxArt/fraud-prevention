# Example output (fictional product)

This is a sample of the skill's report shape. The company below is made up. Do not treat the gap list as a review of Banrox or of any real bank.

## 1. Scope

Product: a consumer account with login, a waitlist for a card, and a household grouping feature. No card has been issued in this example. Payments are not live in the example. Facts the reviewer did not have: processor name, KYC vendor, case-management tool.

## 2. Top abuse cases (for this fictional scope)

1. Account takeover of a shared household account
2. Synthetic or stolen identity at signup
3. Promo or waitlist farming if any reward is attached
4. Social-engineering of support to change a recovery email

## 3. Checklist status (excerpt)

- Unique verified contact: partial
- MFA on password change and payout destination: missing
- Identity required before any money movement: not applicable (no movement yet)
- Case log with time, user, signal, decision, reason, owner: missing

## 4. Gaps, in order

1. Write down what the account can do before a card exists, and lock everything else.
2. Put MFA on recovery and email change even if the card is still in early access.
3. Keep a single case log, even a spreadsheet, from day one.

## 5. 30/60/90

- 30: freeze risky actions, MFA on recovery, case spreadsheet
- 60: written identity policy and vendor list
- 90: first true-positive / false-positive review meeting

## 6. Open questions for counsel

Whether household grouping changes who is a customer for CIP purposes. Do not invent an answer here.
